# Board Memo Q3 2026

## Financial summary
Q3 2026 revenue was $14.8 million, up 38% year over year. Annual recurring revenue reached $61 million at quarter end. Gross margin was 78%.

## Fundraising
The company plans to raise a $75 million Series C in Q1 2027, led by an existing investor. The board approved engaging bankers in November 2026.

## Acquisition target
The leadership team recommends acquiring Lumen Analytics, a data-lineage startup, for approximately $22 million in cash and stock. The codename for the deal is Project Kestrel.

## Headcount
Headcount will grow from 212 to 260 employees by the end of 2027, with most hires in engineering and sales.
