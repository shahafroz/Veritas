# Helix Platform Product FAQ

## What is Helix Platform?
Helix Platform is a managed data-observability service that monitors data pipelines for freshness, volume and schema anomalies. It connects to Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift and Postgres warehouses.

## Pricing
Helix is sold in three plans. The Starter plan costs $499 per month and covers up to 50 monitored tables. The Growth plan costs $1,999 per month and covers up to 500 tables. Enterprise pricing is custom and includes SSO, audit logs and a dedicated success manager.

## Service level agreement
Enterprise customers receive a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If uptime falls below 99.9%, customers receive a 10% service credit; below 99.0%, the credit rises to 25%. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident.

## Data residency
Customer metadata is stored in AWS us-east-1 by default. EU customers can choose eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) at no extra cost. Helix never copies raw row-level customer data; it reads only aggregates and schema metadata.

## Support
Starter customers get email support with a 2 business-day response target. Growth customers get a 1 business-day target. Enterprise customers get 24/7 support with a 1-hour response target for Severity 1 issues.
