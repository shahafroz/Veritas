# Expense and Travel Policy

## General principles
Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be submitted in Ramp within 30 days with an itemized receipt for any purchase over $25.

## Travel booking
Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance when possible. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires VP approval.

## Hotels
The nightly hotel cap is $250 in most cities and $350 in high-cost cities: New York, San Francisco, London and Zurich.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance while travelling is $85. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners.

## Client entertainment
Client entertainment is capped at $150 per attendee and must list every attendee and their company in the expense report.

## Home internet
Employees may expense up to $60 per month for home internet.
