# Information Security Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and systems that store or process Helix or customer data.

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company systems, and hardware security keys (FIDO2) are required for production access. Passwords must never be reused across systems.

## Data classification
Helix data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data, such as compensation, board materials and customer credentials, may only be shared with named individuals and must never be pasted into third-party AI tools.

## Device security
Laptops must use full-disk encryption and auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release.

## Access reviews
Access to production systems is reviewed quarterly by the security team. Access for departing employees must be revoked within 4 hours of their last working day.

## Reporting security incidents
Suspected security incidents must be reported in the #sec-incidents Slack channel or to security@helixsystems.example within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate on your own.

## Vendor security
Any vendor that processes customer data must have a SOC 2 Type II report no older than 12 months and must sign the Helix Data Processing Addendum.
