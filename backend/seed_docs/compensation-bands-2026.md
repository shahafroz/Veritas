# Compensation Bands 2026

## Engineering levels
Software Engineer II (L3) base salary band: $135,000 to $165,000. Senior Software Engineer (L4): $170,000 to $210,000. Staff Engineer (L5): $215,000 to $260,000. Principal Engineer (L6): $265,000 to $320,000.

## Equity
New-hire equity grants vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Annual refresh grants are awarded in the October review cycle.

## Bonus
The company bonus target is 10% of base salary for L3 and L4, and 15% for L5 and above, paid annually in March based on company performance.
