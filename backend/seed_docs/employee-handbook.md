# Helix Systems Employee Handbook

## Working hours and remote work
Helix is a remote-first company. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern Time, Monday to Friday. Employees may work from any country where Helix has a registered entity: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and India.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31. Helix also observes 11 company holidays.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Learning budget
Every employee has an annual learning and development budget of $2,000. It can be used for courses, certifications, conferences and books. Requests above $500 need manager approval in the HR portal.

## Equipment
New hires receive a laptop of their choice (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad X1) and a one-time $750 home-office stipend. Equipment must be returned within 14 days of leaving the company.

## Performance reviews
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Promotions are decided by a calibration committee in the October cycle only.
